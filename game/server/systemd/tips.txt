The Escritoire is a memory game that permanently multiplies a bee mutation's odds by 1.5. It is the least-used block in the pack.
Open the quest book and find the party tab. Party members share progress, so four people in one party fill a bar four times faster.
Waystones: you must physically visit one before you can travel to it. One waystone gives you an empty menu. Two is the minimum.
Trophies drop on Looting, not on patience. Looting III is about 1 in 80. No Looting at all is 1 in 2000.
Wild beehives are two and a half times more common than they used to be. Break one with a Scoop and you have a princess.
The Wyvern Lair is an entire dimension behind a portal nobody has built. Wyverns drop eggs. Eggs become flying mounts.
A princess has an 8% chance to drop twice. Two breeding lines beats one by more than double, because you can cross them.
Put four crops around a cross-crop. AgriCraft will show you a result faster than any other breeding system here.
Thaumic Horizons will clone anything alive that isn't a player. Warded Syringe, Curative Vat, four Victus. Nobody has tried it.
Loot bags contain loot bags. That is deliberate.
Odd Jobs bounties reset every hour. "I only have twenty minutes" is a perfectly good reason to log in.
A bounty's timer starts when you claim the reward, not when you finish it. An unclaimed bounty is a bounty that isn't refilling.
Silver gear comes out of the crafting grid already enchanted, at no extra cost. Smite III and Bane of Arthropods III.
Infernal mobs are the best source of gear on this server. The more words in the name, the better the payout.
There is a quest line in your book that looks empty. It fills in behind you, already completed, after you have done the thing.
Butterflies and bees pollinate leaves. Keep an apiary in a forest and your trees roll mutation dice while you are mining.
Nature's Compass tells you which direction a biome is, and how far. It is sitting in the Discovery quest line.
The Great Work is server-wide. One person finishing a stage completes it for everyone, including whoever is asleep.
Nether fortress chests were rebuilt from scratch. They are worth crossing the lava for now.
Use a Grafter on any leaf block that looks off-colour. That is a new species of tree.
You cannot craft a spawn egg for a creature you have never killed. The recipe asks for what it drops.
A lead helmet is a respirator. Wear one deep underground and foul air stops eating you.
